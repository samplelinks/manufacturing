Information we collect

When you contact us, make a trade enquiry or apply for a job, we receive the details you choose to give us, such as your name, email address, phone number, location, message and, for job applications, your CV and related details.

How we use it

We use your information only to reply to you, handle your enquiry or application, and keep records required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

Your information is shared only with the people in our group who need it to respond to you, and with service providers who help us run this website and our email, under appropriate confidentiality.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and applications only for as long as needed for the purpose they were sent, or as required by law, and then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by writing to support@kalimark.net. We will reply within a reasonable time.
